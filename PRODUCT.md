# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

React (owner-specified). Vite + React SPA with client-side routing. Styling approach delegated to the build (no CSS framework pinned).

## Users

- **Individuals seeking guidance** — people at life decision points (career, health, relationships, marriage timing) who want a personal kundli and a consultation.
- **Couples & families** — families arranging a marriage who need kundli matching (gun milan) and muhurat selection.
- **Home & business owners** — people buying, building, or struggling in a home/commercial space who want vastu consultation and practical remedies.
- **Spiritual seekers** — people exploring vedic astrology, numerology, gemstones, and remedies as ongoing practice.

## Product Purpose

An astrology consultancy website. It presents the consultancy's services (kundli creation, kundli matching, vastu consultation, personal astrology consulting, and related offerings), publishes astro-education content on a blog to demonstrate expertise, and converts visitors into booked consultations across four contact channels (WhatsApp, phone, contact/booking form, booking flow placeholder).

## Positioning

Not yet articulated by the owner — open decision. The site currently competes on service clarity and trust; a distinctive positioning statement is pending.

## Operating Context

Consultations are delivered personally (chat/call/meeting), not through a self-serve product. The site is the front door: visitors arrive from search, referrals, or social, evaluate services, and initiate contact. All four contact channels are wanted simultaneously (WhatsApp chat, click-to-call phone, contact/booking form, online booking placeholder).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Confirmed services: kundli creation, kundli matching, vastu consultation, astrology consultant services. Additional common astrology offerings (numerology, gemstone recommendation, remedies, muhurat) may appear as sample content but are not yet confirmed services.
- Blog exists for "astro information" — educational/explainer content.
- Brand name: **placeholder in use** — owner will supply the real name later; it must be trivially swappable.
- Contact details (phone number, WhatsApp number, address) are placeholders.
- Pricing, testimonials, credentials, and client stories are undecided/absent — sample content must be visibly placeholder, never fabricated as real claims.
- Deploy target undecided.

## Brand Commitments

None yet. Placeholder brand name until the owner commits one.

## Evidence on Hand

None. No real testimonials, client counts, certifications, lineage claims, pricing, or photos exist. Future work must not fabricate any of these as real; placeholder/synthetic content must be visibly labeled or obviously sample.

## Product Principles

1. **Trust through specificity** — astrology clients judge credibility by how concretely and honestly a service is described, not by mystical language density.
2. **Meet the seeker's moment** — visitors often arrive during a life decision (marriage, home purchase, career crossroads); the site should answer "can this help my situation" quickly.
3. **Every path leads to a conversation** — whichever surface a visitor lands on, a low-friction way to contact is always within reach.
4. **Education is proof** — the blog's astro information demonstrates competence and earns the consultation.
